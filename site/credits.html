Older versions (in progress)

The patchers for 1.12, 1.14 and 1.16.5 are being rebuilt by comparing existing Eaglercraft sources with the official game. These are the sources they are compared against; each one's licence is checked and listed with its version before that version is released.

Development tools

Used while building the patchers, never shipped inside a release: JavaParser (Apache-2.0 or LGPL-3.0), SpecialSource, and the Mod Coder Pack mapping data used to read the older Eaglercraft sources. Mapping data and Mojang's files are fetched onto your own computer and are not republished here.

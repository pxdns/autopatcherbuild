Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Short version: this site keeps no accounts and no tracking.

This website

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising. The site is static files.
  • Hosting. It is served by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the page requested) under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub. The Versions page and the build-status bar fetch data from the GitHub API (api.github.com) in your browser, so GitHub sees your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy. Downloads come from GitHub Releases.
  • Your browser. If you press the theme button, your choice (“light” or “dark”) is saved in your browser's local storage. It is never sent anywhere.

The patcher

  • It runs on your computer. Your client jar and what you build stay on your computer and are never uploaded to us.
  • To build, it downloads Mojang's official client jar and mappings directly from Mojang's servers, which see the request as they would for any download.
